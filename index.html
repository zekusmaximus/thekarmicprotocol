The Karmic Protocol

A techno-spiritual thriller

For most of human history, enlightenment took a lifetime of discipline. At a quiet retreat center, a small team has digitized Nirvana.

The Karmic Protocol is a virtual reality experience, built on quantum processing, neural feedback, and artificial intelligence, that permanently dissolves the ego. In trials, it has worked every time.

That’s a problem in an economy that runs on people wanting things. When one of the creators unveils the protocol at a conference, a conflicted corporate lobbyist and Frank O’Hearn, the chairman of a major tech company, bring in the federal government. Homeland Security starts raiding, and O’Hearn uses the raids as cover for a hostile takeover of the project’s backer.

The creators have been building headsets all along, in a chip plant disguised as a solar-panel factory. They and a few allies call themselves the dharmapalas, protectors of the protocol, and they’ve vowed to put off their own enlightenment until everyone else has theirs. Now they have to get the headsets out before the government finds the plant, and then live with what happens to a world that stops wanting.

About the author

Jeffrey A. Zyjeski is a Connecticut attorney and lobbyist who writes speculative fiction at the fault line between technology, power, and spiritual hunger. The Karmic Protocol is his debut techno-spiritual thriller.
